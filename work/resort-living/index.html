Resort Living

Area
250 m²
Scope

Status
Completed

The brief was deceptively simple: turn an ordinary landed house into somewhere that feels like arriving on holiday. Not a themed resort, but the particular calm of a place where the outside is always close by.

An arched pavilion opens the living spaces directly onto the pool and garden, so the boundary between inside and out is barely drawn. Timber, stone and planting carry through the house, and a frangipani tree anchors the terrace it was built around.
