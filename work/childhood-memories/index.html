Childhood Memories

Area
310 m²

Returning to a childhood home is a particular kind of brief. The house already holds a story; the work is to make room for the next one without erasing what came before.

The original roof structure was opened up and left exposed, giving the upper floor height and light it never had. Below, a calm palette of timber, stone and off-white lets the family's own life supply the colour.
