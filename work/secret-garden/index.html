Secret Garden

Location
Novena
Typology
Mixed-use, commercial and residential
Area
3,420 m²
Scope
Architectural
Status
Concept proposal

The studio's largest proposal to date, and the one that tests its ideas at civic scale. Apartments sit above a commercial ground floor on a busy Novena corner.

The move is to hollow out the base. A planted garden is carved through the ground plane and left open to the street, so the building gives something back to the neighbourhood it sits in.
